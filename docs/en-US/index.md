# YundaDesk Help Center

YundaDesk brings customer conversations from your website, Telegram, and other connected channels into one Inbox. AI customer service can respond first, while human agents can take over at any time. The knowledge base, learning review, and AI skills help the AI improve. Yuna helps your team understand data, complete setup tasks, teach the AI, and handle reminders.

## Start here

1. Read [Meet YundaDesk](./01-getting-started/overview.md) to understand AI customer service, Yuna, the Inbox, and the knowledge base.
2. Follow the [15-minute quick start](./01-getting-started/quick-start.md) to connect a channel and complete a real test.
3. Read [Configure AI reception](./04-ai-customer-service/configure.md) to decide which channels the AI should serve.
4. Read [Build the knowledge base](./05-knowledge-and-learning/knowledge-base.md) to give the AI reliable information.
5. Verify answers and their sources in the [Playground](./04-ai-customer-service/playground-and-trace.md).

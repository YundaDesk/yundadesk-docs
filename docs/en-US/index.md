# YundaDesk Help Center

YundaDesk brings customer conversations from your website, Telegram, and other connected channels into one Inbox. AI customer service can respond first, while human agents can take over at any time. Knowledge, improvement suggestions, and skills help the AI improve. Yuna helps your team understand data, complete setup tasks, teach the AI, and handle reminders.

## Get more help

Select **Help & Support** at the bottom of the YundaDesk sidebar. Open the Help Center for guides, or choose **Direct support** to start or continue an in-app support conversation. The panel opens at the latest message, including when you reopen an active conversation or return from chat history; after scrolling up, use **Jump to latest** at the bottom of the message area. Select, paste, or drag multiple files into the composer, review them, and remove any you do not want before sending; uploading starts only after you select Send. Closing the panel does not end the conversation or clear unsent text and attachments on the current page, so you can continue editing when you reopen it. A Support reply adds an unread indicator to the sidebar entry; sound and desktop alerts follow your notification settings. Use the end icon in the top-right corner when the request is resolved. While a conversation is active, reopen it from **Support conversation** directly below the AI section; after it ends, you can still review it in chat history.

If you are not signed in yet, select **Need a hand?** on the login page to open the Help Center or Changelog. The Changelog itself is public. When you select Yuna at the bottom, the small window asks you to log in first. After login, you automatically return to the Changelog, workspace Yuna opens with the summary request prefilled, and nothing is sent until you confirm. If you are already signed in, Yuna opens directly instead of sending you to the login page.

# Meet YundaDesk

YundaDesk is an omnichannel AI customer service platform for cross-border brands and support teams. It brings conversations from connected channels into one Inbox, where AI and people can work on the same conversation.

## Four core roles

| Role | Serves | Main purpose |
|---|---|---|
| AI customer service | Your customers | Answers with knowledge, skills, and customer context, then hands off when needed |
| Yuna | You and your team | Answers business questions, helps with setup, organizes learning, and surfaces tasks |
| Inbox | Your support team | Handles conversations, customer context, AI takeover, and team collaboration |
| Knowledge base | AI customer service | Stores trusted product, policy, service, and FAQ content |

A workspace can have multiple customer-facing Agents for responsibilities such as pre-sales or after-sales support. Each Agent has one Role and its own knowledge, capabilities, response behavior, and channels. A channel is handled by no more than one Agent at a time. External Agents can reply, but they do not participate in YundaDesk managed learning.

## How a customer question flows

1. A customer sends a message through a connected channel.
2. If the channel is bound to an enabled Agent, that Agent answers using its knowledge, capabilities, and customer context.
3. Your team can inspect the answer sources and handling details in the Inbox.
4. The conversation moves to a person when the AI lacks a reliable answer, the customer asks for a person, or a handoff rule applies.
5. Strong agent answers and explicit AI corrections can become learning suggestions. They affect the AI only after review and adoption.

## What to do first

Start by connecting a channel, creating and configuring an Agent, adding knowledge, and using **Test conversation** on the **AI Agents** page. Bind more channels and use proactive outreach only after core answers are reliable.

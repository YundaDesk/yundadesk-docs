# Read the AI reception report

Use reception reports to evaluate AI and human service. Confirm the time range and channels before comparing metrics.

## Main metrics

- **AI-handled conversations:** Conversations where AI participated during the selected range.
- **Automatically resolved:** Conversations completed without human handoff under the product definition.
- **Human handoff:** Conversations transferred from AI to a person.
- **Satisfaction:** Calculated only from received customer feedback. With no feedback, it should show no data rather than 100%.

## How to read the report

Do not read percentages without sample sizes. Combine metrics with failed conversations, skill use, channel delivery, and customer feedback.

## When numbers differ

If numbers differ, check the range, time zone, channel filters, and metric definition. Aggregated reports may update later than live conversations.

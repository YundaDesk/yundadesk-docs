# Set the language and workspace time zone

Administrators can change the interface language and workspace time zone under **Settings → General → Language & timezone**.

The interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), and Italian. You can also switch languages on the sign-in page or from your avatar menu. Each language appears under its native name. The interface changes immediately and keeps your choice after refreshing the same browser. Select **Save** in General settings to save your language and time-zone preferences.

Changing the interface language does not translate existing customer messages or change your AI agent’s reply language. Some help articles and product updates remain available in Chinese or English.

The selector recommends your current browser time zone and supports search by city, common country or region, IANA time-zone name, and UTC offset. Options show their current UTC offset, and the selected option shows its local time. After you select **Save**, reports and workspace times use the saved time zone.

If your team works across regions, choose the shared time zone used for business reporting and schedules rather than one member's local computer time.

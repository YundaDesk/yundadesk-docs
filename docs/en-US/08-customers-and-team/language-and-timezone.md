# Set the language and workspace time zone

Administrators can change the interface language and workspace time zone under **Settings → General → Language & timezone**.

The selector recommends your current browser time zone and supports search by city, common country or region, IANA time-zone name, and UTC offset. Options show their current UTC offset, and the selected option shows its local time. After you select **Save**, reports and workspace times use the saved time zone.

If your team works across regions, choose the shared time zone used for business reporting and schedules rather than one member's local computer time.

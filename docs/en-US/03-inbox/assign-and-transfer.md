# Assign and transfer conversations

A conversation can be handled by AI or transferred to an authorized team member.

## Transfer to a person

1. Open the conversation.
2. Select the take-over or assignment action.
3. Choose yourself or another available member.
4. Confirm the new reception state at the top of the conversation.

After a person takes over, AI should not continue replying unless the conversation is explicitly returned to AI.

## Return to AI

Confirm that the issue is suitable for automation, the channel is enabled for AI reception, and no human promise is still pending.

## Troubleshooting

If a member cannot be selected, check their status and permissions. If ownership keeps changing, refresh and inspect the system transfer records in the conversation.

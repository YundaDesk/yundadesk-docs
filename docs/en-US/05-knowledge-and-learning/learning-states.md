# Understand improvement suggestion states

An improvement suggestion is only a proposal awaiting confirmation. Adopted content is managed from its related page.

## States

- **To confirm:** Waiting for your decision.
- **Adopted:** The suggestion created or updated related content. Use the destination shown in the suggestion list to continue managing it.
- **Ignored:** Will not take effect and should no longer appear as pending work. You can return it to review later.

Open **AI Agents → Improvement suggestions** to search customer questions or proposed answers and filter by To confirm, Adopted, or Ignored. Each suggestion identifies its target Agent. The action explains whether adoption adds knowledge, creates a skill draft, prepares outreach, or saves customer memory. Draft content still requires the review and activation steps shown on its destination page.

Only reusable knowledge, content, or handling gaps become improvement suggestions. Tool, connected-app, and delivery failures remain in the related Agent, skill, or outreach record for page-guided diagnosis or retry; they are not automatically converted into learning.

Risk is not a lifecycle state. Suggestions that affect policies, commitments, customer actions, or sensitive information show a risk indicator and require stricter review.

## Review before confirmation

Before confirming, check the customer question, suggested answer, source, scope, expiry, and required features. Do not confirm a suggestion that lacks a reliable answer or depends on an unconnected app.

## Duplicate suggestions

Equivalent intents should merge into existing learning content or a skill. If ignored content repeatedly returns, preserve examples and report it instead of processing every duplicate.
